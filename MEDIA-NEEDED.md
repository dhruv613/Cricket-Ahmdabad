# Photos to complete the academy gallery

The website now uses six images rendered from its 3D campus: outdoor nets, a practice lane, the ground in daylight, the ground under floodlights, a match-ground overview, and the outdoor fitness area. These are labelled as model views. Indoor cricket content has been removed.

Please provide these real academy photos when available:

- **Coaching:** a coach working with a player on batting or bowling technique, plus any coach portraits you want featured. The model cannot show your actual coaching staff.
- **Training:** players practising in the outdoor nets, fielding on the ground, or doing fitness drills. Model athletes illustrate activity but cannot document actual sessions.
- **Matches:** action from a real match, a team photograph, and tournament or celebration moments. The model shows the space, not actual events or results.
- **Optional facility photos:** wide shots of the outdoor nets and ground, including one under floodlights, to replace the illustrative renders with the real site.

Landscape originals work best for the gallery. Include the coach or event name and a short caption when relevant. No indoor photos are needed.
